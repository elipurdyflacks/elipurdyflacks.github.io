NOTABLE PROJECTS & CREDITS

Sound Designer & Composer — Growth (Documentary Film)  |  Dir. Dominic Russo

2024 – 2025

  • Sound designed and composed original music for the feature-length documentary Growth, which follows comedian Bryan Bishop's decade-long journey living with a brain tumor. All music and sound other than dialogue in the film was made by manipulating and sampling MRI sounds from Bryan Bishop’s brain scans.
  • Growth received a glowing review from Film Threat (Cinequest 2026) and won the Audience Award for Best Documentary at the Cinequest Film & Creativity Festival (2025) which was voted America's Best Film Festival by USA Today readers.

Composer & Speaker — Sonic Futures Multichannel Works  |  Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts, New York City

June 13, 2024

  • Presented an original multichannel sound work entitled Anthropocene as part of the Sonic Futures Multichannel Works event, held inside Bina48's Afro Future Garden at Lincoln Center — a major commission by Grammy-nominated artist Nona Hendryx (founding member of LaBelle).
  • Anthropocene was one of ten original compositions featured in The Dream Machine Experience, Lincoln Center's first-ever mixed reality installation, combining AI, AR, and VR with Afrofuturist art and music.
  • Delivered a public address before the live audience at Lincoln Center, speaking to the conceptual significance of the piece that explores nature, technology, and Afrofuturism through four interconnected sonic worlds inspired by the work of Octavia E. Butler and scholar Tina Campt.
  • The event and broader Dream Machine project were covered by Artnet News, Berklee College of Music, and the Lincoln Center Press Room.
